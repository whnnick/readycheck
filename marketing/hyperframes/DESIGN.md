## Style Prompt

ReadyCheck product promo preview for GitHub README. Use a dark macOS premium canvas, real product screenshots, restrained blue and green accents, liquid-glass depth, and clean product-focused motion. The result should feel like a polished desktop utility showcase, not a marketing splash screen.

## Colors

- Background: #07101E
- Surface: #111923
- Foreground: #F7FAFF
- Muted text: #A7B0BE
- Blue accent: #0A84FF
- Green accent: #35E599

## Typography

- Primary: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "Segoe UI", sans-serif
- Use bold weights only for product labels and metrics.

## Motion

- Product screenshots stay readable at all times.
- Use short entrance motion, subtle floating, and light sweeps.
- Do not overlap the three product modules.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not crop product screenshots into half-visible blocks.
- Do not put cards on top of each other.
- Do not add GitHub URL text; the README already provides project context.
- Do not use purple gradients, large abstract blobs, or generic neon decorations.
